Use `99 - System Definition/Claude/Scratchpad.md` to externalize state during long or complex tasks.

**Why:** Context rot degrades quality in long conversations. Writing intermediate results, decisions, and progress to the scratchpad means the conversation can be compacted or restarted without losing track of where things stand.

**How to apply:** At the start of any multi-step task, write a brief plan to the scratchpad. Update it at natural milestones. Don't wait until the end.
